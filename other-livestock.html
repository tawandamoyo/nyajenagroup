Rabbits & Goats

Profitable Small Livestock Systems

Nyajena Agricultural Group empowers farmers to diversify beyond poultry with hardy rabbit and goat breeds that excel in Zimbabwean environments. We provide breeding trios, grow-out stock, and the technical support required to manage intensive or semi-intensive setups.

From urban youth projects to commercial meat operations, our programmes ensure low feed costs, strong growth rates, and consistent market access.

Rabbits

  • New Zealand White, California, and Chinchilla breeding stock.
  • Housing designs for battery, hutch, and deep litter systems.
  • Breeding calendars, kindling management, and record sheets.
  • Feed formulation using locally available forages and pellets.

Goats

  • Boer, Kalahari Red, and Mashona goats for meat and breeding.
  • Semi-intensive housing with pasture rotation guides.
  • Health protocols covering deworming, dipping, and vaccination.
  • Breeding herd selection and kid nutrition plans.

Unlock Local Market Demand

We connect farmers to butcheries, restaurants, and export buyers seeking premium rabbit meat and goat carcasses. Our specialists help with classification standards, cold chain planning, and contract structuring to guarantee repeat orders.
