About Us

Nyajena Agricultural Group was founded by Zimbabwean farmers who saw the need for a reliable, farmer-first supply chain for poultry and small livestock. We have grown from a single hatchery distribution route into a nationwide network delivering healthy stock, tailored technical advice, and market access to producers at every scale.

Across peri-urban hubs, communal lands, and commercial estates, we champion modern biosecurity, data-driven production, and resilient farmer incomes. Our team blends veterinary science, agribusiness planning, and on-the-ground experience to keep your projects profitable.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted livestock partner empowering African farmers to produce safe, traceable protein that feeds communities and fuels economic growth.

Our Mission

  • Deliver healthy poultry and livestock backed by rigorous biosecurity.
  • Equip farmers with actionable training, data, and field support.
  • Develop resilient markets and fair value chains for producers.
  • Invest in rural talent, innovation, and climate-smart agriculture.

Our Values

  • Integrity in every contract, delivery, and advisory session.
  • Stewardship of animal welfare and environmental resources.
  • Partnership that treats farmers as collaborators, not customers.
  • Continuous improvement driven by farmer feedback and market data.

Why Choose Nyajena Agricultural Group?

  • 10+ years managing supply chains that keep farms running on schedule.
  • Dedicated veterinary and technical specialists in every region.
  • Flexible order sizes with financing options for vetted farmers.
  • Market linkage to processors, retailers, and institutional buyers.

Ready to take your farming to the next level?

Partner with Nyajena Agricultural Group for dependable livestock supply, tailored support, and routes to market that keep your agribusiness growing.
