Other Poultry

Diversify Your Poultry Revenue

Tap into premium and niche protein markets with hardy guinea fowl, broad-breasted turkeys, and dual-purpose duck breeds from Nyajena Agricultural Group. We build each programme around your target market—be it hospitality supply, festive season demand, or community-based protein schemes.

Birds are supplied with biosecurity support, tailored nutrition guides, and vaccination plans that accommodate mixed species setups without compromising health outcomes.

Guinea Fowl

Known for strong disease resistance and lean meat, our keets adapt quickly to free range systems. We provide housing templates that limit flight, feed formulations with high protein starter, and predator management tips.

Turkeys

Broad-breasted whites and bronzes are available as poults or growers. Expect structured feeding regimes that hit 6-8 kg dressed weights just in time for holiday orders, plus vaccination cover for fowl pox and Newcastle disease.

Ducks

Pekin and Muscovy ducks provide dual-income streams through meat and eggs. We guide you on pond or trough water systems, litter management, and breeding rotations to keep production rolling year-round.

Market Development & Support

  • Cold chain and slaughter guides for seasonal turkey sales.
  • Recipe development and packaging ideas for gourmet markets.
  • Contract farming opportunities with hotels and retailers.
  • Record keeping templates for mixed species production.
