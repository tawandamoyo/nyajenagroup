Layers

Egg Production You Can Count On

Our layer pullets and point-of-lay birds are selected for consistent egg size, shell quality, and peak lay sustainability. We source from breeders that keep parent flocks on precision nutrition, delivering birds with high persistency through the 100-week cycle.

We complement each flock with feed phase charts, lighting programs, and vaccination schedules that keep production curves stable regardless of housing system.

What We Offer

  • 6 to 8 week old pullets ready for grower housing.
  • 16 to 18 week point-of-lay birds with full vaccination history.
  • Brown and tinted egg strains suited to cage or free range.
  • Layer feed blends, calcium boosters, and vitamin packs.
  • Nesting and perch system design for various flock sizes.

Management Support

  • Egg collection protocols to reduce breakages and contamination.
  • Lighting programs for open-sided and environmentally controlled houses.
  • Production dashboards tracking hen-day percentages and egg mass.
  • Veterinary diagnostics for coccidiosis, IB, and other layer diseases.

Plan for Market Demand

Nyajena Agricultural Group helps align your flock size with wholesale and retail egg demand. We analyse pricing trends, peak holiday consumption, and input availability to keep margins predictable, even when grain markets shift.
