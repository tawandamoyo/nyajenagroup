Day-Old Chicks

Strong Starts for Productive Flocks

At Nyajena Agricultural Group, we supply Ross 308 and Cobb 500 day-old chicks that have been expertly hatched, vaccinated, and graded for uniformity. Every batch is lifted directly from our biosecure hatchery, transported under monitored conditions, and accompanied by a brooding plan designed for Zimbabwean climates.

Whether you are raising 200 birds or scaling to 10,000, our day-old chicks give you the genetic potential, livability, and early vigor needed for profitable meat and egg production.

What We Offer

  • Ross 308 and Cobb 500 broiler day-olds.
  • Hy-Line Brown and Lohmann Brown layer chicks.
  • Compulsory Marek’s, Gumboro, and Newcastle vaccinations.
  • Brooding starter pack with feed, vitamins, and disinfectants.
  • Transport crates sanitized and sealed for each delivery.

Why Farmers Choose Our Chicks

  • Consistent chick weights between 40-44g for quick brooding uptake.
  • Mortality rates below 1.5% when paired with our brooding guide.
  • Dedicated WhatsApp support during the first 14 days.
  • Flexible delivery schedules aligned with your house preparation.

Brooding Success Toolkit

As soon as you confirm your booking, our technical team shares a pre-delivery checklist covering litter management, temperatures, chick drinker preparation, and emergency power planning. On delivery day, we help you record initial weights and show you how to trend daily mortality, water consumption, and feed intake.
